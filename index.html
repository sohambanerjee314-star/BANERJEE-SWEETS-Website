Our Heritage

From Arambagh with Love

In the heart of Arambagh, what started as a small humble shop near the Hospital More has now become a landmark of purity and taste.

At Banerjee Sweets, we believe that 'Mishti' isn't just food; it's a celebration of life's most precious moments. Our recipes have been passed down through three generations, ensuring that the Rossogolla you eat today tastes exactly like the one our grandfather first perfected.
